# MPPA case: political and legal environment in 2020

Research date: 5 November 2026. This is an evidence review for discussion; it does not change the presentation or classify SWOT factors.

## Main finding

The existing Political factor is government pandemic-containment policy: regional PSBB implementation and restrictions on movement and operating hours. A separate Legal factor is supported by explicit disclosures concerning Law No. 11/2020 on Job Creation, enacted on 2 November 2020. Political and Legal overlap conceptually; avoid presenting PSBB twice as two independent developments.

A consistent presentation convention would be:

- Political: Government pandemic policy changed the conditions under which physical retail could operate.
- Legal: The 2020 Job Creation Law introduced a regulatory transition affecting employment and business requirements; the reports did not establish a quantified final impact.

These describe the external environment. They do not establish MPPA weaknesses, explain all of its losses, or select a strategic response.

## What deserves a place in the case

Retain PSBB under Political using the policy/operating-access lens. A distinct Legal entry can be justified by the Job Creation Law because MPPA, Hero and Ranch explicitly flag it. State that employment and business requirements were undergoing change, with impact still being assessed. If the case needs a quantified legal factor tied more directly to operating costs, minimum-wage requirements are a candidate, but a store-region wage series and MPPA exposure would be needed before using a company-wide percentage.

Tax reform belongs in this overview of the climate, but its link to the working problem is weaker than operating access or employment obligations. Trade/franchise licensing is background context unless a specific material 2020 change is verified. Do not reinstate shopping bags or add generic food-safety, privacy or licensing claims merely to fill the Legal category.
