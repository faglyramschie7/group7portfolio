# Group 7 MPPA Case Study

An interactive study and HTML presentation about PT Matahari Putra Prima Tbk, built with Vue without a build step.

The hosted Vue app opens directly to the presentation at `/study/`. The written study is available at `/study/notes`. Old links to the site root carry their query string and section link forward. For the standalone offline version, open `mppa-case-study.html` in a browser. In the standalone version, select **Notes** for the detailed analysis or **PowerPoint** for the presentation. Select **Ideas Board**, then the **Environment (Food Waste)** horizontal tab, for sticky notes with possible solutions, overseas examples, evidence gaps, and sections to revisit. Use **EN / ID** to switch the study and presentation between English and Indonesian; the browser remembers your choice. Arrow keys navigate slides; **Present** opens fullscreen.

In the PESTLE evidence slide, select **Technological**, then **2020 channels** or **Reach & downloads**. Both evidence views include an external-context takeaway. PESTLE describes external conditions and sector relevance; SWOT classifications, internal capability judgments and response proposals are reserved for later analysis. Download dates and platform differences remain labelled.

The digital channel overview compares documented 2020 ordering, delivery and loyalty channels from company annual reports and a dated Transmart account. Notes include report page references and MPPA channel coverage. Unsupported adoption figures and later planned features are omitted.

The project includes local store images, logos, a Vue runtime, the case extract, and research evidence. Presentation content works offline; external source links require internet access.

## Sources and scope

The analysis uses the textbook case baseline of 2020 and external evidence from 2020–2021. Image captions identify later illustrative material. Detailed citations and limitations appear in Notes and `sources/mppa-pestle-evidence.json`. PESTLE uses Indonesian institutional and industry sources, including Bank Indonesia for macroeconomic consumption and Google, Temasek, and Bain & Company’s *e-Conomy SEA 2020* report for digital consumer adoption and post-pandemic retention.

The full textbook, sample presentation, workbook template, and earlier review materials are excluded from this repository. The textbook link in Notes is available only when that reference is present locally.

Images and company logos belong to their respective owners. Research reports retain their original attribution. Vue is distributed under its MIT license, included in the runtime file header.

Five Forces follows PESTLE on slides 10–11. Select any force in the overview to inspect its evidence, interpretation, possible sales mechanism and limits. Notes and `sources/mppa-five-forces-evidence.json` retain references. Industry scope is consumer grocery retail; qualitative ratings synthesize MPPA and competitor 2020 annual reports and Nielsen’s original April 2020 consumer research. Sales mechanisms are separated from margin effects and causal attribution.

Research constraint: do not use USDA as a direct or indirect analytical basis. Prefer original company disclosures, Indonesian official/industry sources and original market research; retain the observation date and scope of each claim.

Five Forces evidence rule: competitor disclosures establish industry conditions; MPPA disclosures establish its stance against those conditions. Do not transfer a competitor’s sourcing practices, capital spending or operating capabilities to MPPA.

Presentation style: use charts for meaningful numeric comparisons. Separate percentages describing different behaviours into separate charts. Keep slide copy direct and concise; retain detailed source limitations in Notes rather than repeating defensive caveats on each slide. Five Forces includes MPPA sales bars, distribution-capacity bars and separate Nielsen cooking/eating-out donuts.

Editorial rule: state findings directly. Remove audience instructions, repeated cautions, unrelated facts and duplicate explanations. Competitor facts must explain a specific market condition relevant to MPPA. Five PESTLE factors cover customer access, spending, digital channels and environmental resource use. Environmental uses Bappenas’s 2021 study of 2000–2019 national food loss and waste; it links perishable inventory to potential margin and cash benefits without quantifying MPPA savings. Response ideas remain on the Ideas Board for later evaluation. Detailed research qualifications are collapsed in Notes.
